Deep Bite update notes

Everything that changed in the game, newest first · Play Deep Bite · Strategy guide

Deep Bite is a small game that gets patched often. This page is the running log of what we changed and why, written as we go. Balance numbers quoted here are the live values; if a figure in the guide ever disagrees with this page, this page is newer.

Fish art: the eye finally sits above the mouth

OCTOBER 21, 2026

  • Every fish's jaw now hinges in front of and below the eye, and the lower jaw drops instead of the whole mouth opening like a wedge. Before this, an open mouth overlapped the eye on all ten skins — a drawing mistake from the first version, not a design choice.
  • Eyes are a little further back with a thin outline, so they stay readable on the bright skins (Lemon, Koi).
  • A faint gill line separates the head from the body.
  • The icon and the social share image were redrawn to match, with a small fish fleeing in front of the open mouth.

Phones: virtual joystick and a HUD that stops overlapping

OCTOBER 18, 2026

  • Touch controls replaced: touch anywhere and a joystick appears under your finger; let go and your fish keeps its heading. BOOST is a dedicated button on the right, and a second finger anywhere also boosts.
  • Mobile HUD rebuilt: mass and rank in one pill at the top left, a compact "Leaders" list at the top right, the kill feed and minimap repositioned so nothing overlaps in portrait, landscape or on narrow (360 px) screens.
  • Start screen now explains the controls for the device you are on, and the page has a proper How to play, Controls, Tips and FAQ section below the game.
  • Share image and icon added so links look right in chat apps.

Ads, continues and a steadier population

OCTOBER 17, 2026

  • After being eaten you can watch a short ad to come back nearby with half your mass (once per life). A full-screen ad may show before "Swim again" every few deaths, never more often than every two minutes.
  • The game pauses properly while an ad is on screen and resumes where it left off, including when the tab is in the background.
  • Online oceans keep their computer-controlled fish even when the player who was running them leaves — another client picks them up, so a room never suddenly empties.
  • Controls hint on the start screen: steer with the mouse, hold click or Space to boost.

Deep Bite 1.0

OCTOBER 17, 2026 · FIRST RELEASE

  • Shared online oceans for up to 120 fish each, with new oceans opening as they fill.
  • Offline "Play vs Bots" mode with 34 computer fish.
  • Ten fish skins, random fish names, a top-10 leaderboard and a minimap.
  • Three kinds of food (plankton, shrimp, minnows), golden pellets when a fish dies, boost that costs mass, and slow decay above 300 mass so the biggest fish has to keep eating.
  • Rooms that are quiet get filled with computer fish until real players arrive.
