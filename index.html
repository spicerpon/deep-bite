    Deep Bite — a free multiplayer fish .io game

    Deep Bite is a browser game in the spirit of slither.io and agar.io, moved underwater. You start as a tiny fish in a big shared ocean with other players. Everything smaller than you is food; everything bigger than you is a reason to turn around. Eat, grow, climb the leaderboard, and try not to end up as somebody else's snack.

    There is nothing to install and no account to make. Pick a fish, type a name (or keep the random one), and press Play. It works on desktop and on phones.

    How to play

    1. Eat plankton and shrimp — the small drifting things are free food. Plankton gives +1 mass, shrimp +2, minnows +3.
    2. Hunt fish smaller than you — swim into a fish you outweigh by 25% or more and you swallow it, taking most of its mass. Its leftovers scatter as pellets anyone can grab.
    3. Avoid fish bigger than you — the same rule works against you. If a fish is 25% heavier than you, it can eat you. Watch the number under each fish.
    4. Boost to escape or to strike — hold the mouse button (or Space) for a burst of speed. Boosting burns a little mass, so use it when it counts.
    5. Grow and hold the top spot — above 300 mass you slowly slim down, so even the biggest fish has to keep eating. Deaths scatter your mass back into the ocean and you can swim again right away.

    Controls

    Desktop

    • Move the mouse to steer — your fish follows the cursor.
    • Hold the left mouse button or Space to boost.

    Phone & tablet

    • Drag the on-screen joystick to steer. Touch anywhere and the pad appears under your finger; let go and your fish keeps its heading.
    • Hold the BOOST button with your other thumb (or tap anywhere with a second finger) to boost.

    Tips from the reef

    • Early on, ignore other fish and farm plankton near the edges of the map — it's quieter there.
    • When a big fish dies, its pellets are the fastest growth in the game. Dive in, but check who else is coming.
    • Boost in short taps to change direction, not in one long burn. A big fish that chases you with boost loses mass doing it.
    • Being 25% heavier is the whole game. A fish that is almost your size can't touch you — use that to bully from a safe margin.

    FAQ

    Is Deep Bite free?

    Yes. The game is free to play in your browser. It may show ads between rounds, and you can optionally watch an ad to continue after being eaten.

    Do I need an account?

    No. There is no sign-up and no password. Your fish and name are saved on this device only.

    Is it really multiplayer?

    Yes — "Play Online" puts you in a shared ocean with other players in real time. If a room is quiet, computer-controlled fish keep it lively until more people join. "Play vs Bots" is an offline mode that runs entirely on your device.

    Can I play on my phone?

    Yes. Open the same address in your phone's browser. Steer with the on-screen joystick (left thumb) and hold BOOST (right thumb) to dash. Landscape works best.

    Why can't I eat a fish that looks smaller than me?

    You need to be at least 25% heavier. Compare the mass numbers under each fish, not the drawn size — fish grow slower than their mass, so a fish that looks only a little smaller may be nearly your weight.

    What happens when I get eaten?

    Part of your mass goes to the fish that ate you and the rest scatters as pellets. You see your stats and can swim again immediately, or watch an ad to come back nearby with half your mass.

    The game feels laggy or other fish jump around. What can I do?

    Online play sends your position a few times a second and smooths everything in between, so a slow or unstable connection shows up as fish teleporting. Try a different network, close other tabs that stream video, or switch to "Play vs Bots", which runs entirely on your device.

    How does the leaderboard work?

    The Top 10 shows the heaviest fish in your ocean right now, refreshed about once a second. It resets when you die, and there is no all-time ranking yet — your personal best is shown on the result screen.

    Do the skins change anything?

    No. All ten skins are cosmetic. Choose whichever is easiest for you to spot in a crowd.

    What data does the game store?

    Your fish name and skin are saved in your browser. Online mode uses an anonymous session ID and your live position so other players can see you; nothing identifies you personally. Details are in our privacy policy.

    Who makes Deep Bite?

    KOGAMES, a small independent game studio from Korea that also makes mobile games on Google Play. Deep Bite is our first browser game.
